About Grantify.

Making scholarship opportunities easier to discover, understand, and explore.

Who We Are

Grantify is a scholarship information website created to help students discover educational financial assistance opportunities.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to make scholarship information easier to find and understand by presenting useful details in one accessible platform.

Our Mission

To provide students with organized and accessible scholarship information that can guide them in exploring opportunities for their education.
